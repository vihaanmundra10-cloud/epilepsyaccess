OUR MISSION

Addressing Inequality in Epilepsy Care and Educating Others of Seizure First AID

The Problem

Epilepsy is a prevalent neurological condition that often requires long-term treatment, routine follow-up appointments, and access to specialists. However, these treatments are not cheap and often are burdening on underprivileged families. Medicaid aims to lower the cost for such families but New Jersey has the lowest Medicaid reimbursement rate in America. It does not make it better that Neurologists, who typically treat patients with epilepsy, have to use expensive equipment to treat patients with Epilipsy and low reimbursement rates do not cover the cost of treatment effectively.

Along with cost barriers, in New Jersey epilepsy first aid care is highly overlooked. Seizure care and protocol is extremely imporant as it could save ones life. In fact, 1 in 10 people will experience a seizure in their lifetime, making it more likley someone will ever use seizure first aid rather over CPR.

Awareness

Educating communities about epilepsy and healthcare inequality.

Advocacy

Supporting more equitable access to neurological specialists.

Research

Exploring barriers to epilepsy care and identifying solutions.
