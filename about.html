ABOUT THE INITIATIVE

Addressing Inequality in Epilepsy Care

The Problem

Epilepsy is a prevalent neurological condition that often requires long-term treatment, routine follow-up appointments, and access to specialists.

However, many patients across New Jersey face barriers to receiving consistent specialty care.

Some neurological practices do not accept Medicaid due to financial and operational challenges, limiting accessibility for many families.

Awareness

Educating communities about epilepsy and healthcare inequality.

Advocacy

Supporting more equitable access to neurological specialists.

Research

Exploring barriers to epilepsy care and identifying solutions.
