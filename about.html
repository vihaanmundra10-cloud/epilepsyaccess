OUR MISSION

Addressing Inequality in Epilepsy Care and Educating Others of Seizure First AID

The Problem

Epilepsy is a prominent neurological condition that often requires long-term treatment, routine follow-up appointments, and access to specialists. However, these treatments are not cheap and often are burdening on underprivileged families. Medicaid aims to lower the cost for such families but New Jersey has the lowest Medicaid reimbursement rate in America. It does not make it better that Neurologists, who typically treat patients with epilepsy, have to use expensive equipment for treatment and low reimbursement rates do not cover the cost of treatment effectively.

Along with cost barriers, in New Jersey epilepsy first aid care is highly overlooked. Seizure care and protocol is extremely imporant as it could save ones life. In fact, 1 in 10 people will experience a seizure in their lifetime, making it more likley someone will ever use seizure first aid over CPR.

Awareness

We focus on spreading awareness about seizure first aid through hosting free seminars, debunking the signficant amount of misinformation spread about epilepsy, and advocating for the struggles underprivelaged patients face in trying to find epilepsy care.

Advocacy

We support medical clinics, hosptials, and organizations by raising and donating money to them, reducing epilepsy treatment costs for patients that need it the most.

Research

We keep track and simply new research about epilepsy, new medicine for epilepsy, and new treatment plans to keep everyone up to date, help the community understand more about the disease and its pathology.
